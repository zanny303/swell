SWELL authored source summary, not a quote. NASA JPL and Caltech authors trained random forest and neural network regressions to estimate underground geologic composition (such as clay content) from InSAR land deformation in California's Central Valley, and found a significant relationship even when using a quarter of the points. Relevance to SWELL: ground deformation carries information about what the ground is made of. SWELL's radar-derived moisture susceptibility map (millimetres of movement per unit wetness, per cell, from the radar alone) is the same idea in a physically explicit form, and is compared with the independent soil map as a check.
