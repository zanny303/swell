Short-interval multilooked interferograms contain a fading signal that biases velocities, larger over vegetated and cropped land and smaller over urban surfaces. The authors estimate the bias empirically from closure phases of interferograms of different lengths and remove it. Relevance to SWELL: the artifact is land-cover dependent, which matches SWELL scaling the artifact by one minus paved fraction; an alternative correction to compare against when triplets are available.
