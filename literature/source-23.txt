NASA JPL OPERA publishes line-of-sight ground displacement over North America from Sentinel-1 C-band radar at 30 m, one file per date relative to a reference date that moves forward in steps. Positive values mean motion toward the satellite. A recommended mask removes water, unconnected and low-coherence pixels. Relevance to SWELL: an independent radar at a different wavelength. SWELL test T1b asks whether NISAR agrees better with it after the soil-moisture correction than before. Limits: C-band is not free of moisture effects, a different viewing angle, and long-wavelength signals are filtered in the portal view.
