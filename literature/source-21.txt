SWELL authored source summary, not a quote. The link is the NeurIPS 2021 paper on failure modes of the method, already in this corpus. A network is trained on data and on the residual of a governing equation at collocation points, and unknown equation constants can be trained together with it (inverse problem). Known weakness: the physics loss can make optimisation hard and constants can trade off against each other. Relevance to SWELL: swell/pinn.py uses a discrete daily residual of the clay equation, trains alpha, b and T with the network, and reports that alpha and T are only partly identified from a short record.
