Multilooked interferograms with short time spans carry a systematic phase bias that does not cancel when summed, so a time series built only from nearest-neighbour pairs (such as 12-day NISAR pairs) can show false displacement trends. The bias is linked to changing soil moisture and vegetation and shows up as non-zero closure phase. Mitigation: include longer-span interferograms or estimate the bias from closure phase. Relevance to SWELL: NISAR GUNW products are nearest-neighbour pairs, so the bias must be handled; SWELL uses a control-ground regression on wetness rather than closure phase because single GUNW pairs do not provide triplets by default.
