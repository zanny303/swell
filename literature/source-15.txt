A change in soil moisture between two radar acquisitions changes how deep and how fast the radar wave travels in the soil, which shifts the interferometric phase even when the ground does not move. The effect is stronger at longer wavelengths such as L-band, lowers coherence, and leaves a non-zero closure phase when three interferograms are combined. Relevance to SWELL: this is the soil-moisture artifact SWELL estimates on open low-clay control ground and removes before estimating clay movement. A wetter soil can look like motion toward or away from the satellite of millimetres to centimetres.
