SWELL authored source summary, not a quote. Preprint arXiv 2210.10665. Open research code that turns interferometric coherence and closure phase into soil moisture using the De Zan model, with rainfall reanalysis to find the driest image as a reference. C-band Sentinel-1 over bare arid soil; the authors note offsets from the assumed dry reference and over-estimated drying after rain, and name L-band NISAR as a future extension. Inputs: an ISCE SLC stack, ERA5-Land or CSV weather, SoilGrids texture. Relevance to SWELL: the reverse use of the same physics. They treat the moisture phase as signal; SWELL treats it as the artifact to remove. Needs SLC stacks, so it cannot run on GUNW pairs alone.
